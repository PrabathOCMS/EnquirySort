# Pricing Overview

OracleCMS offers three plans:

| Plan | Monthly | Best for |
|------|---------|----------|
| Starter | $49 | Single site, up to 3 editors |
| Growth | $149 | Up to 10 sites, custom domains, staging |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, SLAs, dedicated support |

All paid plans include SSL, backups, and email support. Annual billing saves 15%.

For custom enterprise quotes, contact sales — EnquirySort will route those to the sales list automatically.
