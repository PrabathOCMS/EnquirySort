# Getting Started with OracleCMS

OracleCMS is a content management platform for publishing websites, landing pages, and digital experiences.

## Creating your first site
1. Sign in at https://app.oraclecms.com
2. Click **New Site** and choose a template
3. Add pages from the left sidebar
4. Publish when ready — changes go live within a few minutes

## Inviting teammates
Go to **Settings → Team**, enter their email, and choose an Editor or Admin role. Invites expire after 7 days.

## Supported browsers
Latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. Internet Explorer is not supported.
