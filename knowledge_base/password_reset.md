# Resetting Your Password

If you cannot sign in:

1. Open https://app.oraclecms.com/forgot-password
2. Enter the email on your account
3. Check your inbox (and spam) for a reset link — links expire in 60 minutes
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters

If you never receive the email, confirm with your admin that your user still exists, then contact support.
